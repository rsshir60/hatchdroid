Initial public release of HatchDroid (v0.1.0):
- In-place archive extraction powered by the Rust hatchzip-core engine.
- Zero-overhead unpacking for ZIP, 7z, RAR4/5, and TAR/TAR.GZ archives.
- 16 KB ELF memory page alignment for Android 15+.
- Real-time 3-phase storage telemetry canvas (reclaimed, staged, compressed).
- Dual-progress Foreground Service with notification pause/cancel controls.
- Crash-safe atomic resumption index.
- Smart Pre-Flight Storage Planner with configurable safety buffers.
- Password prompt sheet for encrypted archives with Zeroize memory scrubbing.
- 100% offline, zero telemetry, zero advertisements.
